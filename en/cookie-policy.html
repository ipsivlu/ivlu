Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains which cookies are set when you visit the website of IVLU S.A.S., NIT {{ site.nit }}, {% include direccion.html %}, and how to control them. A cookie is a small file your browser stores to remember information about your visit. It complements our Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics measures visits with the _ga and _ga_* cookies

Google Tag Manager loads Google Analytics, which stores the _ga and _ga_* cookies to tell visitors and sessions apart. We use them to learn which pages and contact channels people use. They last up to 2 years, according to Google's settings.

The Kommo form and Google Maps set their own cookies

  • Kommo: the "Should we contact you?" form loads from forms.kommo.com and may store cookies to work and prevent duplicate submissions.
  • Google Maps: the map of our location on the home and contact pages is served by Google, which may set its own cookies.

These third parties handle those cookies under their own privacy policies.

Turn cookies off in your browser or with Google's add-on

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's privacy settings (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox). To stop only Google Analytics measurement, Google offers the browser opt-out add-on. If you block cookies, the website still works; the form and the map may not load.

Questions about cookies: email {{ site.contact.email }}

For questions about this policy or to exercise your data rights, email {{ site.contact.email }} or call {{ site.contact.phone_display }}. Visitors from the United States can also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice.
